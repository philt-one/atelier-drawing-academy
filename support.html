SUPPORT

How can we help?

Atelier Drawing Academy is a free drawing-tutor plugin. For plugin problems, feedback, accessibility issues, or feature requests, please open an issue in the public GitHub repository.

Open a support request on GitHub ↗

GitHub Issues are public. Do not post personal details, private conversations, unpublished drawings, or sensitive photographs.

Private contact

For confidential support or privacy-related questions, you can contact the publisher by email at atelier.gpt.support@gmail.com.

Common questions

How do I get feedback on a drawing?
Upload your drawing within your Atelier conversation and request a critique. Do not post private drawings to GitHub Issues.

Will Atelier remember my progress?
Progress can be summarized in portable checkpoints. Persistent cross-conversation storage is not guaranteed.

Does it cost anything?
Atelier has no in-plugin purchases, paid lessons, or subscriptions. ChatGPT access may be subject to platform terms or plan limits.
