How can we help?

Atelier is a free drawing-tutor plugin. You can ask for a lesson, submit a photo of your drawing for critique, or request an exercise adapted to your current skills.

Before publishing: add a genuine support contact or verified support request form and a response policy.

Common questions

Will Atelier remember my progress?
Progress can be summarized in portable checkpoints. Persistent cross-conversation storage is not guaranteed.

Does it cost anything?
Atelier has no in-plugin purchases, paid lessons or subscriptions. Access to ChatGPT itself may be subject to platform terms or plan limits.
