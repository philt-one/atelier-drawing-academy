MANIFESTO

Drawing should feel tactile, poetic, and alive — not like filling out worksheets.

Why Atelier

Atelier treats learning as a visual ritual. One weekly ingredient becomes four different studies: construction, shading, watercolor, and plating imagination.

Critique

Upload your drawing and get focused, evidence-based feedback instead of vague praise.

Progress

Each track progresses independently. Watercolor can move forward even if perspective still needs work.

FOUR PARALLEL TRACKS

A curriculum arranged like layered paper on a studio wall.

35%

Drawing fundamentals

Line, observation, construction, proportion, perspective, and confident seeing.

25%

Food textures & shading

Gloss, shell, flesh, shadow, softness, leaves, juices, and believable surfaces.

20%

Watercolor

Transparent pigment, subtle wash, controlled edges, and restrained color atmosphere.

20%

Fine-dining illustration

Plating pages, recipe concepts, annotations, and culinary storytelling through drawing.
