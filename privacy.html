About Atelier

Atelier Drawing Academy is a free, skills-only plugin that provides drawing lessons, exercises, and feedback within ChatGPT. The plugin does not operate its own application server, separate learner database, custom analytics service, or external processing system that receives users’ drawing photographs or conversations.

Information you share in ChatGPT

When you use Atelier, you may provide prompts, learning preferences, progress checkpoints, and photographs of drawings to ChatGPT. Those materials are processed within ChatGPT under the applicable OpenAI terms, privacy notices, and settings. The Atelier publisher does not receive a separate copy of those chats or uploads through the plugin, and does not independently store them.

Website hosting

This informational website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data needed to deliver the site, such as IP addresses and request information, according to its own privacy practices. The publisher has not added custom website analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking cookies.

GitHub Issues and email

If you open a GitHub Issue for support, the information you choose to post may be publicly visible and processed by GitHub according to GitHub’s own policies. Do not publish private conversations, personal data, or sensitive drawings in public issues.

If you choose to contact the publisher privately by email at atelier.gpt.support@gmail.com, the publisher may use the information you provide solely to respond to your request, address support or privacy questions, and maintain reasonable correspondence records related to that request.

Data sharing and retention

Atelier itself does not transfer chat content or user drawing uploads to a publisher-operated database, mailing list, or third-party drawing analysis service. Retention and deletion of ChatGPT conversations are controlled by the applicable OpenAI service and user settings. GitHub determines its own handling of website requests and GitHub Issues. Email correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to manage and answer the request.

Privacy questions

For confidential privacy or support questions, contact atelier.gpt.support@gmail.com. For public bug reports, suggestions, or feature requests, you may also use the support page and GitHub Issues.
